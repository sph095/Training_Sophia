Fairies

A tiny, magical being from folklore, often with delicate wings and a gentle glow. Fairies are known for their playful mischief, their kindness to nature, and their quiet habit of leaving small gifts — a dewdrop on a leaf, a patch of flowers where none grew before.

Mermaids

A mythical sea creature with the head and upper body of a human and the tail of a fish. In folklore and stories, mermaids are often gentle, curious beings who live in underwater kingdoms, sing beautifully, and sometimes befriend or help humans they meet near the shore.

Witches

A person — often a woman in stories — who works with nature, herbs, and a bit of magic. A good witch uses her knowledge to heal, to protect, and to stir up a little wonder in the ordinary world. She keeps a tidy garden, a warm kitchen, and maybe a cat who knows more than he lets on.

  • Digital Financial Inclusion: A stated priority — digitalization of financial inclusion services, including schemes like STHREE SHAKTHI YOJANA (women's WASH loans), which requires robust last-mile digital delivery. Source

3. 5 High-Impact Talking Points (Fact-Sourced)

  1. AI & ML Adoption as a Differentiator: IOB earned a special mention for AI & ML Adoption at the 2024 IBA awards — the CTO has a live, public commitment to scaling AI/ML beyond pilots into production (likely fraud detection, credit scoring, customer service). Lead with how your solution accelerates that specific journey. Source
  2. IT Risk & Digital Fraud Management: A special mention in "Best IT Risk Management" means the CTO is under regulatory and board scrutiny for cyber resilience, especially as the branch network expands. Frame any pitch around security, compliance, and audit-readiness. Source
  3. Scaling Infrastructure for 89 New Branches: Adding ~89 branches and 3 regional offices by FY2026 is a significant network, core-banking, and connectivity undertaking. The CTO is likely managing capacity, latency, and security across a rapidly growing footprint. Source
  4. Tech Talent as a Strategic Asset: IOB won "Best Tech Talent and Organization" — the CTO has invested in hiring and retaining top tech talent. Acknowledge this investment and position your offering as a force multiplier for their team, not a replacement. Source
  5. Digital Financial Inclusion at Scale: IOB is digitizing financial inclusion services (e.g., STHREE SHAKTHI YOJANA for women's sanitation loans) — a mandate tied to RBI's financial inclusion goals. The CTO needs low-cost, high-reach digital channels that work in semi-urban and rural settings. Source
